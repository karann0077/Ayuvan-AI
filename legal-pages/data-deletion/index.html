Request deletion of your data

Last updated: 7 November 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your AYUVAN account, including information you shared through the AYUVAN mobile application or WhatsApp assistant.

Do not include medical details in an email subject line. To protect your privacy, give only the details needed for us to identify your account and verify the request.

How to make a request

  1. Email [privacy contact email] from the email address linked to your AYUVAN account, if one exists.
  2. Use the subject line: AYUVAN Data Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, include the mobile number linked to your AYUVAN account or WhatsApp assistant, including country code, and tell us whether you want to delete your entire AYUVAN account or only a specific record/document.
  4. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full medical reports, or unnecessary health details in the request.

If you only use AYUVAN through WhatsApp and do not have an email linked to your account, send the request from your registered WhatsApp number and follow the deletion instructions provided by AYUVAN, or email us from any address and include the linked phone number. We may ask for additional verification before acting.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge the request and verify that you are authorised to make it.
  • We confirm the scope of the request if it is unclear.
  • We delete or de-identify eligible information from active systems, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.
  • We send a status update to the contact method used for the request, where feasible.

What may be retained

We may retain limited information when necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, honour a do-not-contact request, or establish or defend legal claims. Retained information will not be used for unrelated purposes. Backups may be removed on their normal secure-rotation schedule.

WhatsApp-specific deletion

Deleting your AYUVAN account does not delete messages from your personal WhatsApp application or data controlled independently by Meta. You may manage your WhatsApp data through WhatsApp/Meta’s own controls. We will process AYUVAN-held information associated with your linked WhatsApp identity as described above.

Other privacy requests or a grievance

You may also request access, correction, completion, updating, export, withdrawal of optional consent, or help with a privacy grievance by contacting [privacy contact email]. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
