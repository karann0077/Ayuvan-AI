Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AYUVAN (“AYUVAN”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, protects, shares, retains, and deletes personal information when you use our mobile application, WhatsApp assistant, websites, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

Important health notice. AYUVAN supports health awareness, healthcare navigation, and care continuity. It does not provide emergency response, replace a qualified healthcare professional, diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or change medication instructions.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The entity responsible for processing personal information through the Services is AYUVAN, located at [registered business address, India]. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact [privacy contact email].

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and contact information, such as your mobile number, preferred language, and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Health information you provide, including symptoms, health history, medicines, allergies, care preferences, conversations, and updates to care tasks.
  • Content you submit through the Services, such as messages, voice notes, documents, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and images.
  • Support correspondence, feedback, permissions, consent choices, and deletion or correction requests.

Information generated through use

  • Conversation records, timestamps, interaction choices, notification preferences, and message-delivery status.
  • Structured records generated from your input, such as a symptom timeline, health episode, care task, document-extraction result, or summary. We identify the source and verification status of these records.
  • Technical and security information, such as device/app information, IP address, log events, and fraud or abuse indicators where available.

Information from connected services

If you use WhatsApp, we receive the information Meta makes available to us through the WhatsApp Business Platform in order to provide the conversation you initiate or notifications you have authorised. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account; link an authorised WhatsApp identity; and provide the Services.
  • To understand and organise your reported health concern, maintain your health record, prepare summaries, and support care-plan and reminder workflows you request or authorise.
  • To provide language support, follow-up questions, reviewed health information, healthcare-navigation assistance, and user-requested document processing.
  • To communicate service, account, reminder, and support messages in accordance with your preferences and applicable messaging rules.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use AYUVAN to make autonomous diagnoses, prescribe medicines, alter dosages, or make treatment decisions. We do not treat an AI-generated inference as a confirmed medical fact without an appropriate source and verification process.

4. Consent and your choices

Where required, we seek clear, purpose-specific consent for processing health information, WhatsApp communication, document processing, proactive reminders, and sharing selected records. You may withdraw optional consent or change notification settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed and may limit features that need the relevant information. We will explain the practical impact when applicable.

5. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share only the minimum necessary information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Services, such as cloud hosting, secure storage, communications, analytics/security, and AI or document-processing providers, subject to contractual and technical safeguards.
  • Meta/WhatsApp when you use the WhatsApp channel.
  • Healthcare professionals or providers only when you direct or authorise the specific sharing.
  • Authorities, advisers, or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, or necessary to protect safety, rights, or security.
  • A successor organisation in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset transfer, subject to applicable law.

6. AI and document processing

AYUVAN may use automated tools, including language models, speech-to-text, OCR, and document-processing services, to process content you submit. We aim to send only information necessary for the requested task and use safeguards appropriate to the service. Automated outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate; you should review important information and seek professional advice when needed. We do not use identifiable patient records to train general-purpose models unless we first establish an appropriate legal basis, provide any required notice, and obtain any required permission.

7. Security

We use measures designed to protect information, including encryption in transit, access controls, authentication safeguards, audit logging, secure storage, and operational monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; you should protect your devices, account access, and one-time passwords.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to meet legal, accounting, safety, dispute-resolution, or security requirements, or until you ask us to delete it where deletion is available. Retention may differ by record type. We may retain limited information where necessary to honour opt-outs, prevent fraud, establish or defend legal claims, or comply with law.

9. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, completion or updating of, export of, or deletion of your personal information; withdraw consent; manage notifications; and raise a grievance. To make a request, see our User Data Deletion Instructions or contact [privacy contact email]. We may need to verify your identity before acting. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides rights concerning correction, erasure, and grievance redressal. Read the Act.

10. Children

The Services are not intended for independent use by children unless AYUVAN has implemented an appropriate, lawful child/parent or guardian workflow. If you believe a child has provided personal information without required authorisation, contact us so we can review the request.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process information in locations outside your state or country. Where this occurs, we use safeguards appropriate to the transfer and applicable law.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Services, law, or our practices change. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
