# AYUVAN v0.2.0 — Release Notes

**Type: engineering research prototype; NOT a clinically released product.**

## Delivered

- Preserved six v0.1.0 clinical YAML packages unchanged; introduced versioned v0.2.0 counterparts.
- Mapped the 54 original specialty entries to a reviewable 115-concept symptom/sign/measurement vocabulary. Disease overlap is preserved and is not treated as separate diagnostic certainty.
- Added 43 new draft symptom/measurement-oriented pathways (49 latest IDs total, including global emergency). The new pathways do not constitute fully developed disease algorithms.
- Extended the shared emergency prototype from 11 to 17 candidate rules; still far from comprehensive.
- Added a coverage subsystem with per-condition, per-symptom, per-pathway JSON, CSV, and Markdown reports. No scenario is labeled clinically validated.
- Added explicit `unmapped_symptoms` and `requires_additional_assessment` to the structured result; unmatched concerns cannot be buried by P0 reassurance.
- Fixed generic episode-duration contamination: multi-symptom and ambiguous episodes do not supply a single global `duration_days` value.
- Added new negative, alias-routing, emergency-freshness, condition-mapping, and version/status software regression tests.
- Kept `POST /api/v1/assess` disabled; expanded `GET /api/v1/coverage` for engineering visibility.

## Technical debt that blocks clinical deployment

- Many of the new rules are deliberately simple question/decision placeholders, with generic worsening + functional impact checks and `timeframe: pending_clinician_approval`. These do not replace specialist-authored care pathways.
- Source references are candidate bibliographic pointers. Full primary-guideline rule-to-source traceability is unverified.
- 8 entries are vocabulary-only, 3 are supporting-fact-only, and 5 are safety-fact-only; none constitutes validated assessment. This is visible in the coverage report.
- Lab measurements have no clinically reviewed numeric interpretation. Pregnancy is excluded from routine adult pathways, and child-specific pathways do not exist.
- English is the sole declared pathway question/response language. Hindi/Hinglish vocabulary aliases are NOT validated extraction capability.
- Natural language extraction, privacy controls, user authentication and real App/WhatsApp integration are outside this stand-alone repository.
- Clinical utility and safety are untested against independently clinician-labeled real cases.

## Required next milestone

Move selected pathways through qualified clinician authoring and review, verify every source-to-rule relationship, fill special-population coverage, benchmark the intake/extraction module, and complete legal/regulatory assessment. No pathway is ready to be activated for real patients.
